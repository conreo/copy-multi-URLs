## Privacy Policy for Copy Multi URLs

**Effective Date:** June 2026

Copy Multi URLs does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data
or usage information.

### What data does this extension access?
The extension accesses:
- The text you select on web pages (solely to extract URLs from it)
- The URLs of links you right-click (solely to copy them)

### Where does this data go?
Nowhere. All processing happens locally in your browser. URLs are copied to your
system clipboard via the standard browser clipboard API. Nothing is transmitted
over the network, stored on disk, or sent to any third party.

### Analytics and tracking
This extension contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no
tracking code of any kind.

### Third-party services
This extension does not communicate with any external servers or services.

### Changes to this policy
If this policy ever changes, it will be reflected in the extension's listing on
addons.mozilla.org and in the source repository.
